# <project> — stance

The rules a token cannot express. Written before the screens and amended only with a reason.

## 1. Stance

- What this product believes, in two or three sentences a stranger could test a screen against.
- What it refuses to look like, and why that matters for this audience.

## 2. Voice

- Address form, verb mood, sentence length.
- How numbers, dates, times and units are written.
- Banned phrases, listed literally.

## 3. Composition

- The one gesture each page repeats (a spine, a plate, a schedule gutter) and why.
- What separates sections, what groups them, and what never appears twice.
- Where asymmetry is allowed and where the grid is absolute.

## 4. Open questions

Decisions deliberately deferred, each with the question that would settle it.
