# Hero candidates 4 — five finished objects

Five heroes, each a complete 1440×900 first viewport (nav included, one primary,
proof inside the composition, no labels on the render). Nothing is applied to the
live `01-ana-sayfa`; the boards stay on `_scratch` until you choose. The user
chooses — these are not ranked here.

## 1 · `cand-1-afis.png` — "Kongre afişi"

- **This page is:** a congress poster pasted over the hall.
- **Visual:** Pexels 20733081 by BBSO — a large auditorium from the audience, the
  speaker at the left, cool grade. Vector layers: a 520px `12` and a white ECG
  line across the audience.
- **Strong:** the first five seconds are the date and the scale; the hall is real
  and the numeral is the only graphic; the one red element is the registration.
- **Risk:** the white type lives on the photograph's dark areas, so a different
  crop of the same hall would fail contrast; the `12` covers the audience's right
  side; the poster says "congress" before it says "society" (the society is the
  13px line at the top).

## 2 · `cand-2-kapak.png` — "Dergi kapağı"

- **This page is:** the society's own issue.
- **Visual:** Pexels 415779 by Pixabay — a real ECG print close-up, 620×640, no
  patient, no hand. Vector: none in the image area.
- **Strong:** the cover masthead, issue line, coverline, `Bu sayıda` contents and
  imprint make the home page the publication; the contents double as the routes.
- **Risk:** a full-bleed white cover is a surface change under the paper nav; the
  ECG image is generic (the print's leads are not identified); the red CTA is a
  web button on a print object.

## 3 · `cand-3-levha.png` — "Atlas levhası"

- **This page is:** an anatomy plate with a key.
- **Visual:** Gray's Anatomy (1918), plate Gray490 — heart, lungs and great
  vessels, public domain (Wikimedia Commons), 620×605. No photo of a patient.
- **Strong:** a real historical engraving at plate scale; the 12-group legend is
  the key; the plate title and caption behave like a book.
- **Risk:** the illustration is 1918, not a contemporary clinical image; the 12
  groups are a list beside the plate, not mapped to anatomy; the engraving's own
  English labels are small and only partly legible.

## 4 · `cand-4-topluluk.png` — "Topluluk"

- **This page is:** the society's map, with its people around it.
- **Visual:** Pexels 12319354 (poster session, Roxanne Minnish), 15325468
  (audience, Luis Quintero), 35138560 (convention hall, Tahir Xələfquliyev);
  the Turkey outline is Natural Earth 110m admin-0 (public domain) with 41 dots
  and one Ankara marker.
- **Strong:** the 41 dots and 12 group cards are the proof themselves; the mosaic
  shows the community without a patient; the congress is a single line.
- **Risk:** the poster photo has faces (a candid conversation, not a camera
  smile); the dots are a field inside the outline, not exact province positions;
  the trade-show hall is a generic convention centre.

## 5 · `cand-5-algoritma.png` — "Klinik karar şeması"

- **This page is:** the decision chart a physician already knows.
- **Visual:** Pexels 9408866 by Stephen Andrews — a monitor with an ECG strip,
  220×140, inside the guideline node; the chart is the main object.
- **Strong:** the society's tools are the nodes of a clinical path; the selected
  path is red; the four figures sit in the nodes (member count, provinces,
  groups, guidelines).
- **Risk:** the single primary is `Kılavuzu aç`, not congress registration; the
  chart reads as a diagram more than a hero; the monitor card is the only photo.
