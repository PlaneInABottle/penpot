# Perde — stance

## 1. Stance

The city is the interface. A visitor should never feel they left the map to read a list —
the pin and the row are the same event, and selecting one lights the other.

It refuses to look like a booking dashboard. No dark glass chrome, no neon markers, no
filter panel with twelve controls. Istanbul is not a database.

## 2. Voice

- Turkish, second person singular is avoided; labels are noun phrases.
- Times as `20.30`, dates as `7 Kasım Cuma`, prices as `₺240`.
- Numbers carry the hierarchy: a count, a time or a price is set larger than the sentence
  around it.
- Banned: `dünyanın en`, `eşsiz`, `unutulmaz`, exclamation marks, lorem ipsum.

## 3. Composition

- One gesture repeats: a pin and its row share a selection. Every screen shows that pairing.
- Paper surfaces float on the map; nothing is docked to an edge with a hard rule.
- Asymmetry is allowed on the map (pins sit where venues are); the list is a strict grid.

## 4. Open questions

- Does the figurine pin replace the lollipop pin, or sit beside it as a "featured" state?
- Is the pixel layer a full component language or only the pin and detail card?
